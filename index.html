About

I'm a Hong Kong-based producer and videographer with 7+ years across branding films, documentaries, live events and press photography. Currently Content Creator at HK01, I run full production pipelines end to end — concept, shoot, edit, delivery — and manage budgets from HK$20,000 to 50,000 per project.

My work sits at the intersection of commercial polish and documentary honesty: a CNBC feature on Asian retail, an impact film for Habitat for Humanity, brand activations for M+ and Goodman. In 2021 I was a finalist in the National Geographic Hong Kong Photography Competition (Nature/Wildlife).
